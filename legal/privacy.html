Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

This policy explains how Phillip Konkle (“the operator,” “we,” or “us”) handles information when you use MetaGameTracker Bot (“the Bot”) in Discord servers. The operator is based in Ohio, United States.

1. Information the Bot handles

  • Discord and command information: Discord user, server, category, and channel IDs; command names and timestamps; and whether the user had certain Bot roles when a command was used. Discord may also provide usernames or display names needed to operate a command.
  • Game and event information: player names, archetypes, event dates and names, tournament results, pairings, standings, and information submitted through Bot commands and forms.
  • Store and hub information: server names and IDs, owner names and Discord IDs, store addresses, profile settings, and role assignments.
  • Integration credentials and links: Melee.gg client credentials that a store operator chooses to provide, tournament identifiers, and Moxfield decklist links submitted to the Bot. Melee.gg credentials are used to request tournament data for the store.
  • Files and submission data: CSV, text, and Melee tournament data may be processed through Replit Object Storage as part of a submission. Temporary processing files may be removed after processing; the resulting event records may remain in the database.

2. How information is used

Information is used to provide Bot features, accept and organize game-event submissions, generate reports, manage store and hub settings and roles, connect to Melee.gg or Moxfield when requested, maintain command and error logs, protect the service, and respond to support or deletion requests.

3. Service providers and sharing

  • Discord: provides the platform through which the Bot receives interactions and sends replies.
  • Replit: hosts the Bot and provides Object Storage used for some file-processing workflows.
  • PostgreSQL: the Bot stores operational data in a PostgreSQL database. The operator has not identified the database hosting provider for this policy; this notice will be updated when that provider is confirmed.
  • Melee.gg and Moxfield: the Bot may contact these services when a user requests tournament-data or decklist processing. Those services handle information under their own terms and privacy practices.

We do not sell personal information. Information may be visible to people in a Discord server when a command posts a response there. The Bot operator can access stored data for administration and can use the restricted database-download command to export it.

4. Retention

The operator’s current retention practice is to keep database records indefinitely. You may request deletion by emailing metagametrackerbot@gmail.com and including the Discord ID associated with the records. The operator will review the request and may need additional information to identify the records. Some information may remain in backups or in Discord or third-party systems that the operator does not control.

5. Security

The Bot uses access controls and hosting/database services to operate. No internet service or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. The database provider has not yet been identified in this notice, so provider-specific security details are not stated here. Do not submit passwords or credentials that you are not authorized to share.

6. Your choices and requests

You can stop using the Bot or ask the server administrator to remove it from a server. For access, correction, or deletion requests concerning information held by the operator, contact pkonkle213@gmail.com. For deletion requests, use metagametrackerbot@gmail.com and include your Discord ID. Rights available to you depend on applicable law.

7. Children

You must meet Discord’s minimum age requirements and any higher minimum age that applies where you live. The Bot is not intended for use by anyone who is not legally permitted to use Discord.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the Bot or its data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when an update is made. Questions about this policy can be sent to pkonkle213@gmail.com.
