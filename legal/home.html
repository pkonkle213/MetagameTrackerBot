A practical toolkit

The details that keep a scene connected.

Use the bot to submit and view the information around your game events and community. Supported details depend on what your server chooses to configure and share.

01 / EVENTS

Event information

Submit and view event information, player names, and the details your community uses to follow its tournaments.

02 / RESULTS

Archetypes and tournament results

Keep archetypes and tournament results available alongside community event information.

03 / REPORTS

Reports and related settings

Submit and view reports, with related settings supported for the server’s community setup.

04 / COMMUNITY

Configured roles, store, and hub profiles

The bot can manage configured Discord roles. Store and hub profiles and settings are supported.

Connect the details you already use.

When requested, the bot can connect to Melee.gg for tournament data and Moxfield for decklist links. These are optional service connections, not a claim that every event or decklist is automatically imported.
