Dealing With Offensive Words

Permissions Required - MTSubmitter or owner

I want to be prepared for those who do use this bot inappropriately. If you notice that an inappropriate word or phrase has been used, use the /addword command to add the word or phrase to your banned words.

  1. Use the /bannedwords add command

  1. Add whatever word you want to ban

  1. Once this message is sent, the bot will go through all archetypes added for your store and disable those that contain the word or phrase exactly. As a bad example, let’s say you enter ‘123’, then archetypes ‘123 abc’, ‘1234’, and ‘0123’ will be disabled and won’t be visible to the next public commands like /metagame. The owner of the discord will then need to delete the message posted by the bot with the offending word/phrase
  2. The word will be added to a list banned by the store and those who have too many offenses will face a cool down period of a month where they won’t be able to submit archetypes.
  3. When a new archetype is submitted, the bot will ensure that this word/phrase is not used and will be rejected.
