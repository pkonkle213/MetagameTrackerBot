Personal History - Pairings

Permissions Required - Everybody

This command requires the store to submit pairings data. If standings data is submitted, it will not be reflected in this command

Assuming a player has submitted archetypes for themselves, /history standings gives a detailed recap of the previous 8 weeks. This will give the player the history of what they played, what they played against, and their record for that match. This command has two optional parameters: start_date and end_date to help control what is returned.

To use this command:

  1. Type /history standings in the message bar and select the correct command
  2. Optionally, you may enter the start_date and/or the end_date
  1. If the end_date isn’t provided, it’s assumed to be today
  2. If the start_date isn’t provided, it’s assumed to be 8 weeks since the end_date
  1. Once submitted, the MetaTracker will respond with the history of the player in the category’s game and the channel’s format.
