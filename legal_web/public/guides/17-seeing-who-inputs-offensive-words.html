Seeing Who Inputs Offensive Words

Permissions Required - MTSubmitter or owner

After words have been listed in the store’s database, a store may want to review who has input the word or phrase. Using the /offenders command, an owner or submitter will see the following information printed:

  1. Date the archetype was submitted
  2. The name of the user who submitted it
  3. The userID of the user who submitted it (as names can change on a server)
  4. Event Date
  5. Game (if not specified)
  6. Format (if not specified)
  7. Player Name that the archetype is assigned to
  8. Archetype submitted
