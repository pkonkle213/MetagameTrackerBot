Permissions Required - MTSubmitter or owner

Most events have a way to be run through melee.gg. They have a semi-public API where someone in the organization needs to reach out to their customer service and ask for a ClientID and ClientSecret for their API. You must have provided this information to the bot for this to work. To add them, use the /update command.

If you have your Melee information in the bot, here’s how to get the tournament information.

  1. Identify the event ID that you’d like to upload. The easiest way to do this is to find it in the URL of the tournament page
  2. Back in discord, go to the appropriate category and channel for the upload. Type and select /submit data, and click melee_tournament_id
  3. Enter the tournament ID into the field
  4. Hit [Enter] to send the message and import the data for the entire event into the database!
  5. A modal will pop up to confirm event details such as the date, name, and type.
