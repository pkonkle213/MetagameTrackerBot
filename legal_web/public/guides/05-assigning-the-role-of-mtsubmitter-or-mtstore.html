Assigning the role of ‘MTSubmitter’ or ‘MTStore’

Permissions Required - Owner of Discord or someone who can alter roles

The goal of submitting data is to be as simple and convenient as possible.

  • The MTStore role is required for commands that affect the store directly
  • The MTSubmitter role is required for commands that specifically help track archetype names

These roles are created when you register your discord with the bot and are automatically assigned to the owner of the discord. The purpose of these roles is to allow the store owner to delegate some responsibilities to others in their staff and members of their communities who may be more familiar with their games.

To assign someone a role, there are two methods:

  1. Right click their name in the member’s list, Roles should be second from the bottom. Open that and click “MTSubmitter” or “MTStore” to assign or unassign the appropriate role.
  2. Select the drop down for your store

    Select “Server Settings”

    Select “Roles”

    Select the “MTGSubmitter” role to open it

    Select “Manage Members”

    Select “Add Members”

    Then select the member(s) you’d like to include and then select “Add”
    Without this role, the bot     will not permit you to use certain commands such as /submit data, /view_submissions, /bannedwords offenders, submit archetypes for events older than 14 days, and other cool things!
