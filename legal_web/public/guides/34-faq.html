FAQ:

  1. Why did I get an error saying “The application did not respond”?

This will be the most typical error you’ll see. I cannot control this error, and it likely means that the bot fell asleep and just needs a second to wake up. Please retry the command in a minute and hopefully it’ll work.

  1. Why does the bot require Administrator permissions?

This bot requires Send Messages to send messages to the discord, Manage Messages to delete messages such as submitted data, and Manage Roles to create and assign the “MTSubmitter” role. I am actively working on testing the bot to use these permissions, but python doesn’t currently support this, so the bot will need the Administrator permission for now and may be updated later.

  1. Will other games be supported?

I’m actively working on ways to add other games to the database. To add these games, I just need to know how data can be submitted for them and what formats exist and. I’ll announce any updates in the #updates channel in the bot’s discord

  1. What information is tracked?

As of current state, the only personal information tracked by my bot is a player’s name and a user’s discord ID. I do not track IP addresses, first born child’s names, social security numbers, etc. For stores, only the discord ID is needed. While the bot will save things like the discord’s name, store’s name, and Melee.gg info, all of this is optional.

  1. How do I suggest something new for the bot? I would like more information on __________.

Awesome! I’d love to hear from you. Please use the #store-feedback-and-requests channel to propose these ideas and we’ll have a conversation on how to display and support it!

This channel is only for Store Owners. If you do not have access to this channel and feel that you should, please reach out to me and I’ll grant you access

  1. What if something changes?

I have an #updates channel, where at least once per month (and hopefully more!) I will post any updates I have for the bot. You can see my inner thoughts about the progress of the bot, things I want to see in the future, part(s) of the bot I’m struggling with, or innovations that were made in the name of science!!
