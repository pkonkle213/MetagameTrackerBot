Mapping Formats

Permissions Required - MTStore or owner

Many modern card games have different formats. Lorcana has the Core format, Pokemon has Standard and Expanded, and Magic is filled to the brim with formats such as Standard, Pauper, Legacy, Premodern, Fantasy Standard, Budget Vintage, and more! To map a channel to a game, select /map format from the slash commands.

You’ll be prompted with all formats related to the game that was mapped to the category.

When you select the appropriate format, you’ll be notified that the channel has been mapped.

You can name this channel whatever you’d like, as the ID of the channel has been mapped to the format.

Mapping a second channel to the same format will overwrite the mapping.
