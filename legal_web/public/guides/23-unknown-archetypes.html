Unknown Archetypes

Permissions Required - Everyone

Sometimes players don’t know they haven’t submitted an archetype, and other players are willing to help other players submit their archetypes. Use the /unknown command to find out names and dates for those players.

  1. Type /unknown

  1. The game and format are assumed based upon the category and channel the request comes from and how the channel is mapped. The command has two optional inputs: start_date and end_date

  1. If end_date isn’t entered, it’s assumed to be today
  2. If start_date isn’t entered, it’s assumed to be 8 weeks before end_date, or the last time a ban affected the format
  1. The bot will output the dates and names of people who don’t have archetypes associated with them
