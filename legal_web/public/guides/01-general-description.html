General Description

This Discord bot is built in Python via discord.py with a postgresql database. It's built to be light and community based, bringing discussion into your discord server! I hope to bring data to more people and let them be more informed about what's going on in their magic communities. After installing the bot, you’ll be able to add your results from weekly and larger tournaments, encouraging discussion around your metagame and players!

Throughout this SOP, you’ll see certain things come up:

  • Screen shots with red boxes
  • This is where you’ll need to click or type in information. Other color boxes will be used to bring your attention to details you might miss.
  • Commands
  • These will be represented by formatting like /command. All commands but one are slash commands, sent through the message bar at the bottom of the screen.
  • Discord channels
  • These will be represented by formatting like #channel. They will also link you to the appropriate channel in my discord.
  • “Store”
  • When I reference a “store”, I’ll generally mean a brick and mortar card shop. However, if you lead a local community who meet up and play daily, weekly, monthly, etc, you can also use this! Just have a discord dedicated to your group.
  • “Hub”
  • When I reference a “hub”, I mean a collection of stores that want to track a specific game/format or a region. For example, a hub could choose to track all stores playing Magic in Cincinnati, Ohio, or stores in the Midwest playing Magic’s pauper format.
